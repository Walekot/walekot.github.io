Answers to common questions

How are missing volumes found?

Walekot fills the gaps between the volume numbers you add for each series and edition. Use Mark as Missing to keep a placeholder past your latest volume. The Missing tab lists every gap by series.

Different editions

The same title in another edition language is tracked as its own series, so regional releases never mix.

Archive sheets

Previewing a sheet is free. Creating one uses 1 Volume Credit and saves PNG pages in the Sheets tab. If creating fails or you cancel, no credit is used.

Photos

Imported photos are copied into Walekot as smaller images. Your originals in Photos are never changed, and nothing is uploaded.

Credits

Volume Credits are stored on your device. They can’t be brought back after the app is removed or after Clear All Data.

Contact

Send questions or feedback through the App Store page for Walekot. We read every message.
