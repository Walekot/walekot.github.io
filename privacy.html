Walekot · Manga Shelf Archive

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Walekot helps you catalog the manga volumes you own, see which volumes are missing, and create archive sheet images of your shelf. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected. Walekot works without an account.

2. Information you add

You may add series titles, volume numbers, publishers, edition languages, formats, purchase dates, prices paid, condition notes and photos of covers or shelves. This information is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to us, shared with other users or sold.

3. Camera, Photos, Microphone and Speech Recognition

  • Camera is used only when you choose to take a photo of a cover or shelf.
  • Photos is used when you choose a photo to import, or when you save an archive sheet to your photo library. The app keeps a smaller copy of an imported photo and never changes your original.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition are used only when you choose to dictate text into a field. Speech is converted to text by Apple’s speech recognition service, and the text is shown to you before it is added. Audio is not stored by the app.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device Settings. Declining a permission only turns off the feature that needs it.

4. Notifications and tracking

Walekot may ask for permission to send notifications. Allowing or declining does not change how the app works. Walekot does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and does not include advertising or analytics software.

5. In-app purchases

Volume Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment card or Apple Account details. The app keeps your credit balance and purchase records on your device so each purchase is counted once.

6. Data security

Your library is stored inside the app’s protected storage on your device and benefits from the security features of your operating system, such as device passcode and encryption. Keeping your device secure helps keep your library private.

7. Backups

If you use your device’s backup service, the app’s data may be included in that backup under the terms of the service you use. We do not have access to your backups.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Edit or delete any volume or archive sheet at any time.
  • Use Settings › Clear All Data to remove volumes, photos, archive sheets, drafts and remaining credits from the app.
  • Deleting the app also removes its data from your device. Images you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them.

9. Children

Walekot is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International users

Because your information stays on your device, it is not transferred to other countries by the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, and continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach us through the support link on the app’s App Store page.
