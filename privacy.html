Walekot · Manga Shelf Archive

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Overview

Walekot helps you catalog the manga volumes you own, see which volumes are missing, and create archive sheet images of your shelf. Walekot works without an account and keeps your library on your device.

2. Information you add

You may add series titles, volume numbers, publishers, edition languages, formats, purchase dates, prices paid, condition, notes and photos of covers or shelves. This information is stored only in Walekot’s private storage on your device. We do not receive, upload or sell it.

3. Camera, Photos, Microphone and Speech Recognition

  • Camera is requested only when you tap Take Photo, to photograph a cover or shelf.
  • Photos access is requested when you tap Choose Photo, and permission to add images is requested when you tap Save Image on an archive sheet. Walekot keeps a smaller copy of a chosen photo and never changes your original.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition are requested only when you tap the microphone next to a text field. Your speech is converted to text by Apple’s speech recognition service, and the text is shown to you before it is added. Walekot does not store audio recordings.

4. Notifications and tracking

Walekot may ask for permission to send notifications. Allowing or declining does not change how the app works. Walekot does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, does not include advertising or analytics software and does not read the advertising identifier.

5. In-app purchases

Volume Credits are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Walekot stores your credit balance and transaction identifiers on your device to prevent duplicate credits.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Settings › Clear All Data removes volumes, photos, archive sheets, drafts and remaining credits from Walekot. Deleting the app also removes its data. Images you saved to Photos remain in your photo library.

7. Children

Walekot is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on Walekot’s App Store page.
